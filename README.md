# Expanded Roster

A **Battle Brothers** mod (made for **[Reforged](https://github.com/Battle-Modders/mod-reforged)**) that lets your company grow beyond 20 brothers.

<img width="2139" height="1576" alt="Character screen with two reserve rows" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/3e1d1768-b956-4de3-92f0-76b97804ad86" />

---

## Features

- **Roster size: 30 brothers** (vanilla: 20)
- **Second row of reserve slots** on the character screen: 18 battle formation slots + **18 reserve slots** (vanilla: 9 reserves)
- The **number of brothers in battle is unchanged** – the extra brothers wait in reserve
- Redrawn character screen background with room for the extra row
- Does **not** change the limits of special origins such as Lone Wolf

---

## Requirements

- Modern Hooks
- Modding Standards and Utilities (MSU)
- Made for Reforged (loads after it if present)

**Not compatible with:** Smart Loot (`mod_smartLoot`)

## Installation

Download the zip from the latest [release](../../releases) and put it (unextracted) into the game's `data` folder.
The mod is **safe to add to or remove from an existing savegame**.
